About

I'm a bridge engineer at Ramboll. I joined Ramboll India in Gurgaon in 2019 and moved to the Major Projects team in Southampton in 2023.

Most of my work is the design and analysis of bridges to the Eurocodes, with a focus on post-tensioned concrete. I also have working knowledge of AASHTO LRFD.

I do much of that work through FE and parametric models: SOFiSTiK and LUSAS for analysis, and Rhino, Grasshopper and Karamba 3D for geometry and automation. A good parametric model pays off on the complex problems and the repetitive ones alike.

I also run SpanLab, a YouTube channel with tutorials on structural engineering software and computational design.

I'm working towards ICE chartership and aim to complete my CPR in 2027.
