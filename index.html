About

I'm a bridge engineer at Ramboll. Most of my work is post-tensioned concrete design, and the FE and parametric models behind it.

I joined Ramboll India in Gurgaon in 2019 and moved to the Major Projects team in Southampton in 2023. I design and analyse bridges to the Eurocodes, and have working knowledge of AASHTO LRFD.

I use SOFiSTiK and LUSAS for analysis, and Rhino, Grasshopper and Karamba 3D for geometry and automation. A good parametric model pays off on the complex problems and the repetitive ones alike.

I also run SpanLab, a YouTube channel with tutorials on structural engineering software and computational design.

I'm working towards ICE chartership and aim to complete my CPR in 2027.
